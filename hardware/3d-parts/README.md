# 3D Printed Parts

Printable parts for this project are planned but not yet designed.

## Goals

- **Electromagnet mounting bracket** — holds the electromagnet at the correct
  distance from the chime gear without drilling into the clock body
- **Cable channel / strain relief** — routes wires cleanly out the back of the clock
- **Cosmetic enclosure** — covers the electromagnet, wiring, and barrel jack
  connector so they are hidden from view when the clock is on display

## Design Notes

- Target printer: standard FDM (PLA or PETG)
- Should attach with existing clock screw holes or friction-fit to the back panel
- Must allow easy removal to service the clock mechanism
- Enclosure should match or complement the clock's wood housing aesthetic
